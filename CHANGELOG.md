# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-05

- Sharpens the image-fiber covering exponent to the constant rank of
  `DF|ker(DH)`, equal to `rank(DF) - rank(DH)` when both ambient ranks are
  constant.
- Completes the regular-tube bootstrap and ambient `C1` flow argument, and
  replaces the transported-law total-variation bound by the uniform
  `tanh(C0 * Delta)` bound.
- Adds accepted/rejected continuation accounting and a three-population
  full-map numerical-rank audit; the latter records the honest negative result
  that the sampled complete Jacobians all have numerical rank eight.
- Adds compact, pickle-free case-level evidence from the completed three-seed
  conditional EDM/U-Net study, with deterministic crossed-bootstrap analysis
  and figure regeneration.
- Separates reused evidence from still-missing nonlinear-tube,
  horizontal-flow, coarea-law, matched-rank learning, and noisy-posterior
  experiments in a preregistered gap plan.
- Refreshes mathematical scope, provenance, reproducibility, integrity, and
  public-release documentation.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-04

Initial public research release.

- Presents the reviewed mathematical theory of regular conditional fibers,
  covering entropy, full-spectrum noisy tubes, horizontal transport, and
  conditional-law stability.
- Includes a version-pinned, pointwise conditional application of an external
  DDPM convergence theorem.
- Provides sanitized numerical EIT results, deterministic analytic examples,
  regression tests, and scripts for regenerating public figures and tables.
- Documents mathematical scope, evidentiary status, reproducibility limits,
  licensing, attribution, and open research questions.
