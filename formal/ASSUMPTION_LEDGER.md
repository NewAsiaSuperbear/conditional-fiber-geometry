## 2026-09-08 incremental assumptions and evidence boundary

- **M15 local substitution:** `T>0`, `ε₀>0`, actual radius `T^(-c)≤ε₀`, and the entropy bound only on `0<ε≤ε₀`. The constant precedes the radius quantifier. No sign of c is imposed in the general theorem. The unit corollary requires `T≥1,c≥0`; the general threshold requires `c>0`. No DDPM score/noise/initialization theorem is imported or postulated.
- **M09 genuine Hessian:** H is C² at the point (or on the stated open region). The Jacobian is definitionally DH and the curried Hessian is D(DH); surjectivity and `s‖z‖≤‖DH z‖` on the kernel orthogonal complement are actual hypotheses, with s>0. The Hessian norm bound L is assumed for H, not supplied as an unrelated operator field. The conclusion is pointwise; a two-point Lipschitz statement still needs segment/convexity or path control. Analytic derivative bounds for a real EIT forward map remain external obligations.
- **M20 two-point score:** q is an NNReal with q≤1. Forecast values are 1 and 2, truth is the actual fair two-point PMF, and the two forecasts have a product law constructed by bind/map. The denominator is the truth absolute value (1 or 2), never a predicted norm. This is a finite-support counterexample, not a universal statement about every scoring rule.
- **Execution:** baseline SHA 8501264 was rebuilt from a clean detached worktree using the existing fixed dependency cache. Its 173 selected targets, CFG namespace audit (608 declarations), and `leanchecker CFG` passed. The unmodified supplied script first failed at network dependency fetch; its original failure record is retained separately. Baseline results do not certify subsequent edits. Each final repair result must use the repair logs and source digest.
- **Tool trust:** cached mathlib/Lean dependencies remain in the trusted toolchain boundary. `leanchecker` replays project declarations in Lean's kernel; it is not an independent mathematical specification verifier. Namespace and source-module inventory are separate checks. No actual EIT data certificate or cold rebuild of every dependency is claimed.

- **Model/support composition:** The covering wrappers assume compact E and globally continuous `model.complete`, together with actual strict derivatives on the relevant fiber and actual composite rank lower bounds. A local open-domain C¹ derivative bridge is supplied, but the global continuity premise of the covering construction has not been removed by a cutoff/extension argument. The support theorem uses an actual probability prior concentrated on E, and concludes almost everywhere for its observed pushforward. Its C₀ is pointwise in the observation, without measurable-selection or integrability claims. The full-rank finite branch and the nonempty Dirac/identity instance are proved separately. The 3→2→1 example uses a closed ball, not the radial annulus demanded for simultaneous transport/coarea assumptions.

- **Continuous ranks:** finite nonempty coordinate type, actual probability Measure, permutation invariance, a.e. injectivity, and measurable comparisons give uniform rank. The real iid corollary assumes an actual atomless probability law and constructs the product-law/permutation/no-ties/measurability bridges. Approximate conditional samplers, correlated draws, and rounded ties are not automatically covered. Only the finite rank pushforward is equated with a uniform PMF measure.

- **Extrema interval:** truth is coordinate 0 and the n remaining coordinates are samples from the same real atomless iid law. The event requires both a sample no greater than truth and a sample no less than truth; under no ties this is precisely internal rank. It is not a theorem about arbitrary intervals or approximate posterior samplers. The n=16 probability is exactly 15/17.
